Still Moment is the meditation app for your own recordings: import meditations from your MBSR or MBCT course, from podcasts or from websites, and listen offline. Plus a meditation timer for silent meditation with singing bowl and gong — ad-free, no subscription, no tracking.

YOUR PERSONAL MEDITATION LIBRARY
Collect the meditations that do you good: the body scan from your mindfulness course, self-compassion practices, the recordings of your teacher. Everything stays private on your device, with no tracking — nobody is watching. And it's always at hand, even without internet.
• Import MP3 and M4A files — from your files or shared directly from the browser
• Import podcast episodes — share meditations from Apple Podcasts with Still Moment
• Quick search — by title or teacher
• Preview before you start — jump to any point in a recording
• Trim — set where a recording begins and ends
• Optional gong at the start and end of a meditation
• Pause and resume from the lock screen

YOUR TIMER FOR SILENT MEDITATION
Shape the meditation timer to feel right for you. Start it, put your phone down — the gong tells you when your time is up, even with the screen locked.
• Duration: 1 to 60 minutes
• Adjustable preparation time
• Singing bowl or gong for start, end, and intervals
• Separate volume for gong and background sound
• Background sound: silence, rain, forest sounds, or your own

CALM, CLEAR, WITHOUT DISTRACTION
Still Moment is intentionally simple. No points system, no statistics, no notifications pulling you out of stillness. The app should feel like a pause — not like another task.

YOUR MEDITATION, YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
Still Moment collects absolutely zero data. Everything stays on your device, where it belongs.
• No tracking, no analytics, no ads
• Meditate without internet — everything stays on your device
• No account, no sign-up, no subscription
